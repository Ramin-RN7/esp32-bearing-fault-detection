# Evaluation protocols

Generated by `python scripts/evaluate_protocols.py` (bearing_audio 0.1.0, commit `7017a9eac5fd`). Recording-level results use mean aggregation over the 2-second windows of a recording. Fault F1 treats *fault* as the positive class. Section 1 reproduces the thesis; sections 2–3 are a follow-up evaluation of the two selected thesis pipelines, not part of the original thesis experiments.

## 2. Leave-one-bearing-out (60 recordings, 20 bearings)

**Design (protocol v1).** Each of the 20 folds holds out one bearing (its 3 recordings) for testing. Three other bearings are the validation set, and all models train on the remaining 16 bearings. Only the GMM uses the validation set, to set its decision threshold; the MLP and the baseline ignore it, so all three models train on the same data. The validation bearings are the next three IDs after the test bearing. Bearing IDs are grouped by fault type, so in 12 of 20 folds the validation faults all come from one type. Section 2c varies this choice. Every fold, threshold and held-out score is in `protocol_comparison.json`.

**Uncertainty.** The 95% intervals come from resampling the 20 bearings (10,000 resamples of the fixed out-of-fold predictions). They leave out the variation from refitting the models and from the earlier choice of pipeline settings.

## 3. Repeated matched splits (200 per protocol, thesis sizes 42/9/9)

*Recording* splits shuffle recordings within each class, as the thesis did. *Bearing* splits keep all recordings of a physical bearing in one subset. Both use the same seeds, but they select different test recordings: on average the two test sets share 1.4 of 9 recordings. Per-split results are in `repeated_splits.csv`.

| Pipeline | Protocol | Balanced accuracy, mean ± SD | 10th–90th percentile | Fault F1, mean ± SD | Splits with F1 ≥ 0.9 |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A8 GMM | recording | 0.766 ± 0.152 | 0.50–0.92 | 0.849 ± 0.096 | 41% |
| A8 GMM | bearing | 0.767 ± 0.155 | 0.50–1.00 | 0.833 ± 0.131 | 40% |
| A16 MLP | recording | 0.800 ± 0.143 | 0.58–1.00 | 0.863 ± 0.098 | 48% |
| A16 MLP | bearing | 0.790 ± 0.141 | 0.58–1.00 | 0.838 ± 0.125 | 48% |
| Baseline: RMS + kurtosis | recording | 0.776 ± 0.124 | 0.58–0.92 | 0.795 ± 0.122 | 27% |
| Baseline: RMS + kurtosis | bearing | 0.794 ± 0.122 | 0.67–1.00 | 0.792 ± 0.160 | 39% |

The SD is the spread between whole random splits, not a confidence interval. Difference between the protocols (recording minus bearing, mean balanced accuracy), and how the archived split ranks among the recording-level splits:

- A8 GMM: 0.000; 26% of random splits score at least the archived split's 0.917
- A16 MLP: +0.010; 57% of random splits score at least the archived split's 0.833
- Baseline: RMS + kurtosis: -0.018; 99% of random splits score at least the archived split's 0.500

These differences are below 0.02, so the comparison shows no large average inflation from splitting by recording instead of by bearing. It compares averages over different test sets and has no equivalence margin, so it does not prove that bearing overlap is harmless, or measure its effect on the archived split.
