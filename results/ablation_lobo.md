# Every ablation pipeline on unseen bearings

Generated by `python scripts/evaluate_ablation.py` (bearing_audio 0.1.0). A 2026 follow-up, not part of the thesis. Each pipeline is rebuilt from its archived run record: the run's own feature code on the archived two-second segments, and the model stage from its settings. Decisions are per recording (mean of the window scores unless the pipeline aggregates otherwise). Balanced accuracy (BA): 0.5 is chance.

86 pipelines: the 82 window-level configurations and the A40/A41 aggregation variants of A8 GMM and A16 MLP. The other 7 ablation IDs are not separate pipelines (see the JSON). On the original thesis split, **84 of 86** rebuilt pipelines reproduce their archived confusion counts exactly.

## 3. Choosing the pipeline without seeing the held-out bearing (nested selection)

Picking the top row above uses the same 20 bearings to choose and to score, so its BA is optimistic. Here each outer fold chooses among all 86 pipelines by leave-one-bearing-out on the other 19 bearings only, then tests the chosen pipeline on the held-out bearing.

- **Nested BA: 0.950 [0.89–1.00]**, fault F1 0.962, against 0.963 for the best single pipeline chosen with hindsight.
- Pipelines chosen across the 20 outer folds: A5-MLP (12), A2-MLP (4), A7-MLP (3), A38-MLP (1).
- Nested selection minus the RMS + kurtosis reference: +0.088 to +0.287.

## Limits

- 20 bearings from one test rig. The intervals leave out refitting variation; differences of a few recordings between pipelines are within noise.
- Ball-fault recordings were made without load (dataset description), a possible shortcut cue.
- Rebuilt pipelines that do not reproduce their archived counts are still evaluated as specified by their settings; see section 1.
