# Results

Everything here is generated by the scripts in [`scripts/`](../scripts/); none of it is edited by hand. The thesis itself reported results on the original 9-recording test split only. The leave-one-bearing-out, bootstrap, repeated-split and nested results are a 2026 follow-up, not part of the thesis.

| File | What it holds | Made by |
|---|---|---|
| [`ablation_runs.csv`](ablation_runs.csv) | Every logged thesis ablation run (104 rows, 93 configurations): settings as recorded, confusion counts on the thesis test split, and notes where a run's code differs from its log | `build_ablation_table.py` |
| [`protocol_comparison.md`](protocol_comparison.md) | The two thesis pipelines (A8 GMM, A16 MLP) and a two-feature baseline: thesis split, leave-one-bearing-out with every fold, bootstrap intervals, ten other validation choices, no ball-fault bearings, 200 repeated splits | `evaluate_protocols.py` |
| [`protocol_comparison.json`](protocol_comparison.json) | The same, with every held-out score, threshold and the run's provenance | `evaluate_protocols.py` |
| [`repeated_splits.csv`](repeated_splits.csv) | One row per repeated split, protocol and pipeline (1,200 rows) | `evaluate_protocols.py` |
| [`ablation_lobo.md`](ablation_lobo.md) | All 86 ablation pipelines rebuilt and tested on unseen bearings: rebuild check, ranking, the thesis ranking against it, nested selection, robustness of the leaders | `evaluate_ablation.py` |
| [`ablation_lobo.csv`](ablation_lobo.csv) | One row per pipeline: thesis-split scores and rank, leave-one-bearing-out scores and interval, how often nested selection chose it | `evaluate_ablation.py` |
| [`ablation_lobo.json`](ablation_lobo.json) | The same, with every held-out prediction, fold, nested choice and the run's provenance | `evaluate_ablation.py` |

Balanced accuracy (BA) is the mean of the hit rates on healthy and on faulty recordings; 0.5 is chance. Fault F1 treats "fault" as the positive class. With two fault recordings for every healthy one, a classifier that always says "fault" gets F1 0.800 but BA 0.500, so BA is the main measure here.
